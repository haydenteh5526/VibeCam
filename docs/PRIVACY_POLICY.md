# VibeCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

This policy describes the offline iPhone release of VibeCam. It covers taking photos and short videos, importing photos, applying camera looks, and saving or sharing the results.

## Photos and videos stay on your device

VibeCam develops photos and video clips on your iPhone. This release does not upload them to a VibeCam server or an AI provider and does not require an account. It does not include advertising or app analytics.

## Permissions

- Camera access lets you take photos and record videos. You can import JPEG or PNG files and use Film Roll without enabling the camera.
- Microphone access lets you record sound with a video. If you deny it, videos are silent.
- Add-only Photos access lets you save results to your photo library. Denying this permission does not prevent keeping an item in Film Roll.
- The file picker grants access to the image you select. VibeCam does not scan your other files.

## Storage and deletion

Film Roll stores photos and clips, including their originals, in the app's storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. Clips are limited to 15 seconds. Save a copy to Photos if you want it outside the app.

Deleting an item from Film Roll removes its app-owned media, thumbnail and original. It does not delete copies already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere. Removing the app removes its local Film Roll; separate copies and device backups follow your device and backup settings.

## Saving and sharing

Save adds a copy to your photo library with your permission. Share opens the iOS share sheet; you choose the recipient or destination app. Services you choose, including any photo-library syncing or device backups you enable, handle their copies under their own settings and policies.

## Support

If you contact support, the email address and content you send are used to respond. Include media only if you want to share it for troubleshooting.
